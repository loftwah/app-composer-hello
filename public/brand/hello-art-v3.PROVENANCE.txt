Original App Composer cut-paper artwork — Hello World
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-hello-v3", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a147db-f5a8-7001-82d5-84423a57de6b. The request itself, its prompt, the cost basis and the
review of every rejected image are recorded in the owning App Composer repository at
public/brand/apps/provenance.json and .artwork/tiles-direction-a.json. FAL_AI_API_KEY is read
from the environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed, never written to a
manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction: App Composer cut-paper screenprint — warm ivory paper #f4f0e8, graphite ink
#252923, burnt orange #d95022 as the arch, with one small permission-green square. Flat shapes,
ink-lined paper edges, coarse screenprint grain, deliberate registration offset. No shading,
perspective, gloss, gradients or glow. Subject: a plain burnt-orange doorway arch standing on a
graphite ground rule with the opening left as bare ivory paper; one bold graphite route line with
a single right-angle bend steps up to the opening, passes through it and ends in a solid arrowhead
on the far side, and a small permission-green square sits inside the opening near the ground. The
route stands for a first deployment that actually completes; it is never presented as a
screenshot, a dashboard or a deployment log.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- hello-art-v3-master.png — the reviewed master, square (1024px); byte-identical to the reviewed
  .artwork/masters/tile-hello-v3.png recorded by App Composer.
- hello-art-v3-480.webp and hello-art-v3-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero derivatives, one
  square tile with a reserved 1:1 layout box and a 1px hairline border.
- hello-share-v3.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative. The same bytes are this app's card in the
  App Composer catalogue (public/brand/apps/hello-share-v3.jpg there), so the product page and
  the catalogue describe the app with one card.
- hello-share-v3.webp and hello-share-v3-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP variants.
- apps/hello-small-v3.webp — 96px navigation crop of the same square artwork, alongside the other
  four apps' crops for this app's own app switcher.

Dimensions are reserved in the markup. The illustration is explicitly separate from the deployed
page: it is never presented as a screenshot of this app or of any inspected page.

mark.svg and icon.png are authored, not generated: hand-written SVG geometry of the arch, route
line, arrow and permission square in the same paper grammar, plus a 512px flat ivory rendering of
that same mark for apple-touch-icon. Before this release both files were byte-identical to App
Composer's own mark and icon, which made the reference app wear the parent product's logo; this
release gives it its own. No traced third-party mark, vendor icon or platform logo is used.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the 14 October 2026 glossy
hello-share-v2.jpg card and its provenance JSON, which baked the words "Hello, world. Your first
app with App Composer" into the image itself, and the unreferenced share-ai-v1 poster pair. Their
bytes, prompts and the original generated master path remain in this repository's git history;
the reviewed App Composer record keeps the comparison note.
