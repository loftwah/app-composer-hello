Original App Composer illustration — Hello World
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-hello-v5", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a14913-759f-7640-927a-f3262eae2ce5. The prompt, the full job record and the export commands are
recorded in the owning App Composer repository at .artwork/tiles-v5.json,
.artwork/masters/tile-hello-v5.result.json and .artwork/export-v5.sh. FAL_AI_API_KEY is read from
the local environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed, never written to a
manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction (supersedes the rejected 17 October cut-paper set): original adult-animated science
fiction with confident dark ink contours, hard-edged cel shading in two flat tone steps, drawn
perspective and one clear focal event. Scene: a vast dark launch plain where one tiny, newly
assembled relay — bolted and taped together, with a little dish and one lamp — fires its first
bright orange signal straight up past giant distant towers and a huge moon until a small satellite
lights up in answer. The machine is deliberately dwarfed by the space around it. A small snail-like
critter watches from a rock. Entirely illustrated; no photograph, no 3D render, no glow haze and no
text baked into the image.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- hello-art-v4-master.png — the reviewed master, square; byte-identical to the reviewed
  .artwork/masters/tile-hello-v5.png recorded by App Composer.
- hello-art-v4-480.webp and hello-art-v4-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero derivatives with a
  reserved 1:1 layout box.
- hello-share-v4.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative, composed with deterministic typography from
  the reviewed master. The same bytes are this app's card in the App Composer catalogue.
- hello-share-v4.webp and hello-share-v4-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP variants.
- apps/hello-small-v4.webp — 96px navigation crop of the same artwork.

The illustration is separate from the deployed page: it never pretends to be a screenshot of this
app or of any vendor console.

mark.svg and icon.png are authored, not generated: hand-written SVG geometry in the same family
grammar (ivory tile, ink border, one ink subject and one accent — here a small relay dish with an
orange beam), plus a 512px rendering of that mark for the apple-touch icon.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the 17 October 2026 cut-paper
hello-art-v3 master, 480/960 derivatives, share cards, 96px crops and the v1 archway tile. Their
bytes and prompts remain in this repository's git history.
